FAQ

Common questions

Where is my patient data stored?

+

The clinical record is kept in your clinic's own Google Sheet, which you can open and export at any time. Appointments, WhatsApp message history and documents are held in private, access-controlled storage, kept separate for each clinic.

Do my patients need to install an app?

+

No. Everything arrives on WhatsApp, and patients open the portal from a link with a one-time code.

Can I change the clinical form to suit my practice?

+

Yes. Switch off fields you never use and edit every dropdown, without a developer. Past visits are never changed.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

+

Yes. It runs in the browser on any computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install.

What does the AI voice agent do?

+

It calls patients about upcoming appointments. If they can't make it, it offers open slots, books the new time and updates your calendar. Every call is recorded with a transcript.

Who is PatientPad built for?

+

Dental practices in India with one to three doctors, typically seeing 10 to 30 patients a day.
